Refund Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

One-time digital purchases

Every ANGLER unlock — Dive Supporter, the Archive Pass, All Packs and single packs — is a one-time digital purchase that applies to your account immediately.

14-day refunds on request

If you are not happy with a purchase for any reason, email us within 14 days of the purchase date and we will refund it in full. Include the email address on your account. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

After 14 days

After 14 days we refund purchases that did not work as described (for example, a pack that never unlocked). Tell us what happened and we will sort it out.

What a refund changes

A refunded unlock is removed from your account. Your dives, logbook and ratings are unaffected.
