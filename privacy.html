Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version

Free play never leaves your device. If you sign in, we store the minimum needed to run leaderboards and purchases. We never sell data.

What stays in your browser

When you play without an account, ANGLER keeps everything in your browser's local storage on this device: your daily results, streaks, best depths, the logbook, your settings, an anonymous device identifier, and any dive waiting to be submitted. None of this is sent anywhere, except as described under "Daily curve" below. Clearing your browser data, or using "Reset local data" in Settings, deletes it.

Daily curve

When the online features are switched on, finishing the Daily Dive sends the day, the seven prompt ids, the answers you typed, the seconds you used, and your anonymous device identifier to our server so your dive can be scored and placed on the day's curve. The identifier is a random string; it is not tied to your name, email, or address.

If you sign in

Sign-in uses Google through our hosting provider, Supabase. We store your user id and email (to identify your account), the handle and optional flag you choose, whether you have hidden yourself from leaderboards, your purchases, and a copy of your progress so it syncs between devices.

Your handle, flag, depths, rating and league are public on leaderboards. Nothing else about you is. You can hide yourself from every leaderboard, or delete your account and all server data, from Settings at any time. Deleting your account removes every row that identifies you; a Hall of Fame entry you earned keeps the handle alone, with no link to the deleted account.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. Card details never touch ANGLER. We receive a record that a purchase was completed and which product it unlocks.

Ads and cookies

ANGLER may show ads from Google AdSense. Ads only load after you allow them in the consent banner, and never while a timer is running. Ad networks use cookies to choose which ads you see; you can withdraw consent in Settings. Any purchase removes ads site-wide. ANGLER itself sets no cookies.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled we use a cookie-free, privacy-focused service that counts page views and a handful of events (a dive started, a dive completed, a share, an unlock viewed) without identifying you.

Children

ANGLER is suitable for all ages. Accounts and purchases are for people aged 13 and over.
